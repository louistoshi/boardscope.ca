Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the free version?
The free version includes all core boardview features: loading .brd files, schematic PDF viewing, cross-reference between board and schematic, net highlighting, OpenBoardData integration, Voltage Map, Diode Map, Continuity Checker, and Photo Overlay. No account or payment required.
What's the difference between Lifetime and Yearly?
Both give you access to all Pro features. Lifetime is a one-time payment with permanent access. Yearly is an annual subscription that includes updates and support for as long as you're subscribed. Choose Lifetime if you want to pay once and own it forever.
How does the license work?
Each license is tied to one email address. You can use BoardScope on multiple devices with the same email. If you need to transfer a license to a different email, contact support.
Which AI providers are supported?
BoardScope supports Anthropic Claude, OpenAI GPT-4, Google Gemini, OpenRouter (access any model through one key), and Ollama (run AI locally on your machine). You'll need your own API key for paid providers.
Can I get a refund?
Yes. If you're not satisfied with your Pro license, contact us within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. No questions asked.
Do you offer team or volume licenses?
Yes. For teams of 5 or more, contact us for volume pricing. Each team member gets their own license tied to their email.
