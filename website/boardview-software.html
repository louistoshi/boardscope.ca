Boardview Explained

What is boardview software?

Boardview software is a specialized tool for viewing and analyzing PCB layout files (.brd files). It's essential for board-level repair technicians, electronics engineers, and anyone who needs to understand circuit board layouts.

📋

.BRD File Viewer

Open and render .brd, .bvr, .brd2, and other boardview format files instantly. See both sides of the board simultaneously.

🔍

Net & Component Search

Search any net name or component reference. BoardScope highlights every connection across the entire board instantly.

⚡

Voltage Measurement

Record live voltage readings per net. See color-coded pass/fail overlays update the board view in real time.

🗺️

Volt Map Overlay

Visualize measured voltages as a heat map across the board. Spot power distribution issues at a glance.

🔌

Schematic Cross-Reference

Link your schematic PDF directly to the boardview. Click a component on the board and see its schematic symbol.

🤖

AI Diagnosis

Describe a symptom and let AI analyze your board, measurements, and fault patterns to suggest solutions.
