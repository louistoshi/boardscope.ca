Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 29, 2026

Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how BoardScope ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and handles your information when you use our desktop application and website.

1. Data Collection & Usage

BoardScope is designed as a local-first application. When you use the BoardScope desktop application, the majority of data processing happens securely and entirely on your own device.

  • Board Files (.brd, etc): The schematic and boardview files you load are parsed and analyzed entirely locally on your device. We do not upload, read, or store your proprietary board files on our servers.
  • Diagnostic Logs & Measurements: Voltage measurements, diode mode readings, and any custom notes you take are stored locally in your workspace.

2. AI Diagnostic Features

If you choose to use the AI Diagnosis features built into BoardScope Pro, diagnostic text prompts and relevant localized measurements may be transmitted to our third-party AI providers (such as Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or OpenRouter) strictly for the purpose of generating your diagnostic report. Proprietary schematic geometries are not sent to the AI.

3. Application Telemetry

We may collect anonymous, non-identifiable crash reports and basic usage telemetry (such as the operating system version and application launch events) to help us identify bugs and improve the software's stability. This data contains no personal information or proprietary repair data.

4. Contact & Support

If you contact us for technical support, we will only use the information you provide (such as your email address and system logs) to assist you with your request. We do not sell your contact information to third parties.

5. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us
